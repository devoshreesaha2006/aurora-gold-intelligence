Walk-Forward Methodology & Operational Principles
Time-Ordered Evaluation

Chronological progression guarantees zero future data snooping. Data splits adhere strictly to historical settlement timestamps.

Zero Look-Ahead Bias

Parameters and rolling spread statistics are calibrated strictly on training windows and frozen completely prior to out-of-sample evaluation.

Train-Only Parameter Fit

Z-score thresholds, rolling standard deviations, and mean spreads are calibrated solely on historical segments without test data peeking.

Realistic Cost Accounting

Explicit friction modeling: includes MCX transaction fees, STT, GST, stamp duty (₹95/lot round-trip) plus conservative execution slippage (₹15/lot).

Research Disclosure & Model Limitations: Settlement prices are utilized as proxy fills for relative-value simulation. Actual intraday market execution may differ significantly due to bid-ask spreads, order book depth, timing latencies, and physical delivery tender notice periods. Past simulated performance does not guarantee future market returns.
