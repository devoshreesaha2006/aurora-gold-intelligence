How Normalization Works

Transforming disparate contract sizes, quotes and purities into a true apples-to-apples basis

AURORA CORE METHODOLOGY
Normalized Price = (
Quoted Price Lot Size
) × (
1000 Purity
) × Standard Lot (10g)
Standard Base: ₹ per 10 grams @ 995 Fine Gold
1
Input

Raw Quoted Price

Ingest raw Bhavcopy traded price straight from MCX settlements and live L1 tick feeds.

P_raw (MCX Quote)
2
De-scaling

Size Adjustment

Divide by the quote denomination unit to convert the contract to a uniform per-gram quotation.

P_gram = P_raw / Lot Size
3
Metallurgy

Purity Adjustment

Scale 999 purity (GOLDGUINEA) or 995 purity against pure fine gold benchmark.

P_pure = P_gram × (1000 / Purity)
4
Benchmarking

Comparable Basis

Multiplied by 10 grams standard unit to produce the final cross-contract comparable price.

P_norm = P_pure × 10g Standard
