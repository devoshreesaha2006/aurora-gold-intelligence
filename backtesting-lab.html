MODEL NOTES

Timing, costs & limits

This tool reports a historical simulation, not a forecast. Settlement prices are used as fill proxies; real execution can differ.

  • Results appear only when the selected pair has enough eligible non-synthetic history.
  • Signals are evaluated from trailing data and executed at the next settlement.
  • Transaction cost and slippage are applied to each completed round trip.
