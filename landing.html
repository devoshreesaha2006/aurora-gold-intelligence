FAQ

Need more
details?

What AURORA analyzes, what it needs, and how to enter the workspace.

Does AURORA use live market prices?

The available data mode is shown in the application. The backend supports demo-synthetic and staged CSV observations, plus an authorized live-feed adapter when configured. The landing page does not claim a live feed.

Which contracts are compared?

GOLDM, GOLDTEN, GOLDGUINEA, and GOLDPETAL are normalized to a shared fine-gold basis and evaluated across six unique pairings.

Can I upload my own market data?

Yes. The existing workspace accepts MCX CSV files, validates rows, reports diagnostics, normalizes accepted observations, and records import history.

How are unusual spreads reviewed?

Rolling z-scores and empirical percentiles provide historical context. Event review also considers liquidity proxies, days to expiry, estimated transaction friction, and rule explanations.

Does AURORA place trades or provide forecasts?

No. AURORA provides relative-value research and explainable classifications. It does not execute orders or claim predictive accuracy.

Where is my account information stored?

The current demo authentication stores account data in the browser. It is not connected to a server-side account system.
